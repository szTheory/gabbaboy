**Status vocabulary:** **Adopted constraint** comes from owner/project policy; **Recommended** is the selected planning default awaiting phase-specific implementation review; **Verified observation** is an inspected fact with a limited scope, not a product capability. ## Phase 2 adopted planning contract

The 12 adopted decisions in [02-CONTEXT.md](../milestones/v0.1-phases/GB-02-dmg-cpu-bus-and-time/02-CONTEXT.md) are covered by the nine independently reviewed plans. [02-RESEARCH.md](../milestones/v0.1-phases/GB-02-dmg-cpu-bus-and-time/02-RESEARCH.md) records three concrete clarifications: nonresponding cartridge reads are undefined in the hardware reference, so the scoped core returns unsupported bus for absent external-RAM reads; STOP consumes bounded master event time while CPU/divider/timer/internal serial remain frozen; and Mooneye's common font requires separate rights closure or a documented original replacement before fixture admission. Source-qualified timer/serial expectations and unsupported unqualified overlaps remain bounded by the named model/profile.

These Phase 2 directions are now supported by implementation and verification evidence. The five roadmap truths and CPU-01 through CPU-05 passed independent verification; the admitted corpus is limited to three derived headless reporting closures, and original PPU-dependent paths and physical hardware behavior remain unqualified. Revisit only for conflicting primary evidence or an implementation counterexample; preserve source provenance rather than regenerating expectations from the core.

## Decisions still requiring concrete phase evidence

Phase 1 fixes the exact fixture/opcode subset, time/stop/output contracts, DMG post-boot profile, compiler/dependency pins and advertised platform floors. Phase 4 fixes battery layout, concurrent-writer and recovery behavior. Phase 5 fixes sample rate/resampling/filter policy and perceptual evidence limits. Phase 6 establishes actual Playstead consumption needs, remote gates, release credentials and measured budgets. CGB/state planning must resolve model-applicable oracles, RTC formats and snapshot compatibility. These are bounded design tasks, not blockers to completing initialization.

When evidence changes a decision, retain its ID, date the change, link the counterexample and update the affected requirements/roadmap. Do not promote recommendation status from documentation alone. Keep source/API examples, tests and limitation notes synchronized when behavior is implemented.
