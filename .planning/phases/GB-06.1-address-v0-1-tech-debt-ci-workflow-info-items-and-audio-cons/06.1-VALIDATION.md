- **After every task commit:** quick run command for the touched area, plus `actionlint` when a workflow changed
- **After every plan wave:** full suite command
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** full suite green; exact-head required contexts (`required-native`, `fixture-repro`, `preview-package-smoke`) green on the merged head; all six phases `passed` and fresh on committed main
