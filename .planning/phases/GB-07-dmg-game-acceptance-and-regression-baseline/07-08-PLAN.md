Purpose: Without a declared exclusion count, moving a case to excluded would shrink the denominator silently and the suite would still pass.
Output: gbb_case_applicability, --suite/--expect-excluded/--model/--revision, observe-only flag rejection, two negative case files and ten CTest names.
</objective>
