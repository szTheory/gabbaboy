## Accomplishments

- **Bounded reader and role classifier.** Reads mappings, sequences, block scalars and simple flow lists; rejects anchors, aliases, tags, tabs, duplicate keys and multi-line plain scalars. Every `release.yml` job must have a declared trust role, so a new job fails closed until it is classified.
- **Privilege split (P15-01).** Rehearsal producers keep `contents: read` with `persist-credentials: false` and retain the fuzz receipt as an artifact. A `judge` job runs after every producer, checks out judge code from the default branch, treats the rehearsed commit and artifact as data, and runs the policy judge and the trusted fuzz validator. In `release.yml`, `candidate-corpus` and `candidate-performance` are read-only producers, `candidate-fuzz` is a trusted reader of the completed exact-source CI campaign, and `attach-v02-evidence` holds the only write token for that evidence and executes no checked-out code. The platform manifest waits for the attacher.
- **Subject-selected judge code fixed in the live wiring.** `check_v02_evidence` previously ran `tests/scripts/verify-v02-delivery.py` from the tagged checkout, and the manifest and publisher ran the tagged `verify-release-gates.sh` and `verify-published-release-assets.py` with a write token. They now run from a trusted checkout of the workflow revision; the gate runs the delivery entry point from its own checkout with `--judge-root` and the tag as `--source-root`.
- **Executable intent (P15-10).** A bounded shell reader recovers commands with their if, loop, function and group frames, list connectors and `||` guards. A required call counts only if it is reachable, fatal, in the right job, with the exact arguments, including Plan 05 run, attempt and source identity inputs. Substring and count validators in `verify-release-gates.sh` and `verify-release-candidate.sh` were replaced or re-pointed at the trusted paths.
- **Shared producer contract (P15-15).** `produce-release-evidence.sh` has `verify-subject`, `corpus-build`, `collect` and `verify`, with its own `--self-test`. The three duplicated rehearsal tag setups, the duplicated corpus build and the evidence collection use it; the policy rejects inline tag setup.

Plan 16 owns the real hosted reproduction.
- **Retry cost.** Producers cannot see the draft, so a retry rebuilds them; the attacher keeps already attached evidence and only reads it back.
- The new `ci.yml`, release and rehearsal workflows were checked by `actionlint` and the judge, not by a hosted run.
